Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Schenectady Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Schenectady using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Save (605) 291-3542 in your contacts now, and you can reach us at once when something floods
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • A clearly labeled main water valve, in a spot everyone knows, saves time in an emergency
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply

When to Call for Water Extraction in Schenectady

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
