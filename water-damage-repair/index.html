Confirm It Is Dry

How to Tell You Need Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • Laminate or vinyl plank edges are swollen, curled or separating at the seams.
  • Flooring flexes more than it should in the spot that was flooded.
  • Ceiling drywall is sagging, cracked or crumbling after a leak from above.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.

How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • Curing time for paint, floor adhesive and caulk should pass before the room sees water or hard use.
  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Take pictures of damaged materials prior to removal, since the insurer may want them later.
  • Fresh caulk every year around showers, tubs and sinks stops water reaching the wall cavity.
