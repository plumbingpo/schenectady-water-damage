Water trouble is not all equally urgent, and knowing the difference helps you decide what to do first. We have ordered these five common Bronx calls by how fast each one needs attention.

Bursts: Nothing is more time-critical than a broken water line, which keeps pouring until the supply is cut. Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. You cannot stop the rain, but you can move belongings up and keep away from any outlets near the water. Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. For Bronx kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. When we look underneath, we tend to find water lying on the liner, insulation that is soaked or sweating ducts. Given time, that humidity works its way upstairs and the joists start to rot. Our crew clears any standing water, brings the framing down to a normal moisture level and reports the likely cause.

Odors: Last on the list is a stale, earthy odor in a room that once had a leak, which is worth a scheduled visit. Simple Ways to Keep a Bronx Home Dry

Most of the water damage we see in Bronx homes started with something small that gave warning first. A few minutes of checking a couple of times a year catches most of it.

Begin at the hoses. The rubber hoses on washing machines, dishwashers, toilets and ice makers wear out, and braided stainless replacements are inexpensive. Open each sink cabinet and check for water marks, a warped base or a damp odor. At the water heater, watch for corrosion or moisture around the bottom, and flush some sediment out annually. Outside, keep gutters clear and make sure downspouts send water away from the foundation.

Know where your main shutoff valve is and make sure everyone in the house can turn it. Leak sensors placed by the water heater, washer and under sinks cost little and will alert you early. A stain that spreads or a floor that feels soft is worth a call, and Bronx homeowners are welcome to ask us about it.
